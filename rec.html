Alfredo Pasta

Creamy,delicious and saucy Bechamel sauce!

Prep: 10mins

Ingredients

  • Pasta 250g
  • Water for boiling 2Litr
  • salt 1tbsp
  • Butter 3tbsp
  • All-purpose Flour 3tbsp
  • Milk (full cream)
  • Garlic (minced) 3-4 cloves
  • Black pepper powder 1tsp
  • Dried oregano 1tsp
  • Chili Flakes 1/2tsp
  • Grated cheese 1/2cup
